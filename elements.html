About Me

Sam Heidsick

As a graduate with a Bachelor's in English from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro, I am always eager to learn new things. From playing bass, to learning to code, I am always looking for a new skill to develop. Much of my career has been spent occupying the marketing spheres, offering copywriting, copy editing, and managerial guidance for the non-profit and wellness sectors. More recently, I have found myself largely involved with the world of Artificial Intelligence. My first position in AI annotation and auditing was with Innodata, Inc.. There, I was able to dive into the world of AI and learn more about what makes this industry tick. I worked with high profile clients in the industry to ensure all of the AI output was aligning with brand guidelines, upholding ethics and values, and maintaining factual, relevant information. I have stayed in the AI sphere in the time following my initial evaluative role and look forward to what the future holds for marketing, AI, and the unlimited potential of their intersection.

In my free time, I enjoy going on hikes, teaching myself to code, and playing board games with my fiance! If you have any questions, or would like to get in touch, my information can be found on the bottom of this site.
